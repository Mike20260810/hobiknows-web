HOBIKNOWS POLICY

PRIVACY
POLICY.

HOBIKNOWS values your privacy and is committed to processing information transparently and only to the extent necessary.

LAST UPDATED September 8, 2026
00

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the HOBIKNOWS website, how that information may be used, and what rights you may have.

As a general principle, HOBIKNOWS processes only the minimum information necessary to provide its services.

CONTENTS 01. Information We Collect 02. How We Use Information 03. Cookies & Analytics 04. Third-Party Services 05. Data Retention 06. Your Rights 07. Security 08. Children 09. Changes to This Policy 10. Contact
01

Information We Collect

HOBIKNOWS may collect information that you provide directly while using the service, as well as certain information generated automatically when you use the website.

Information You Provide Directly

  • Name entered in the contact form
  • Email address
  • Subject and content of your inquiry
  • Email address provided for newsletter subscription
  • Other information you voluntarily provide

Information That May Be Collected Automatically

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Access time
  • Pages visited and usage activity
  • Information generated through cookies or similar technologies
02

How We Use Information

Collected information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Reviewing and responding to user inquiries
  • Providing newsletters or updates
  • Operating the website and improving its functionality
  • Analyzing content usage trends
  • Maintaining service stability and security
  • Complying with legal obligations

Personal information is used only within a scope reasonably related to the purposes stated above.

03

Cookies & Analytics

HOBIKNOWS may use cookies and similar technologies to improve the website experience and analyze visitor statistics.

Cookies are small data files stored in your browser and may be used to remember your preferences or analyze website usage patterns.

If HOBIKNOWS uses Google Analytics, Google AdSense, or similar analytics or advertising services in the future, those service providers may use cookies or other technologies.

You can restrict or delete cookies through your browser settings. However, some website features may not function properly as a result.
04

Third-Party Services

HOBIKNOWS may use third-party services or platforms to operate the website.

For example, third-party services may provide hosting, web analytics, email delivery, contact form processing, advertising, or similar functions.

These third-party service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. This HOBIKNOWS Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites or services.

05

Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected.

Once the purpose has been fulfilled or retention is no longer necessary, the information may be deleted or de-identified using appropriate methods, unless applicable law requires a separate retention period.

06

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

  • Confirm whether your personal information is being processed
  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request restriction of processing or withdraw consent

You may submit requests regarding these rights through the Contact page.

07

Security

HOBIKNOWS endeavors to implement reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect personal information.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage can guarantee absolute security, and not all risks can be completely eliminated.

08

Children

HOBIKNOWS is not operated for the purpose of knowingly collecting personal information from children who are below the age at which separate consent is required under applicable law.

If you become aware that a child’s personal information has been provided without appropriate consent, you may request its deletion.

09

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time due to changes in the service or applicable laws and regulations.

If material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page and the “Last Updated” date at the top will be revised.

10

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of personal information, please contact HOBIKNOWS through the Contact page.
